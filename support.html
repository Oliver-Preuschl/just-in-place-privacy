Frequently asked questions

My places don't appear on my other iPhone.

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on. Syncing can take a few minutes.

My friend can't accept my invitation.

Send it to the phone number or email address your friend uses with their Apple Account. Invitations to other addresses can't be accepted.

Why aren't visits detected automatically?

Visit detection needs "Always" location access (iOS Settings → JustInPlace → Location). It can also be turned off for a single place in the place's settings.

How do I share places with someone?

Go to Config → Export (JIP), choose the places and send the file. The recipient opens it in JustInPlace. Notes, ratings and visits are not included.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Config → Unlock Pro → Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.
