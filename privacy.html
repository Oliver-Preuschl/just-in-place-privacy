Summary

We do not run servers for the app. There are no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account. We cannot access it.

Data stored in your iCloud

The app stores your saved places, tags, notes, ratings, planned and detected visits, trips and import jobs in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Apple synchronizes this data between your own devices.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location to:

  • show your position on the map and calculate distances and routes,
  • detect visits to places you have saved (only with "Always" access; you can turn it off per place),
  • share your approximate location with friends you choose, if you turn on Live Location (off by default),
  • record which areas you explore while Explorer mode is on (off by default).

Location data is processed on your device. Where it is shared, it is stored in iCloud. You can change the permission at any time in the iOS Settings.

Explorer mode

While Explorer mode is on, the app records which areas you visit and how long you stay there, also in the background, to show them on the map. This data stays on your device; it is not synced to iCloud or shared with anyone. It is deleted when you end Explorer mode.

Calendar

If you use "Import Visits from Calendar", the app asks for access to your calendars. It reads upcoming events only on your device and only while you use this import; it never changes or adds calendar entries. Only the events you select are saved as visits: the event title as the description, the time and the place. Like all visits, they are stored in your private iCloud database. No calendar data is sent to us or anyone else. You can revoke calendar access at any time in the iOS Settings.

Apple services

Map display, place search, routes and address lookups use Apple Maps. For this, search terms and coordinates are sent to Apple and processed under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive this data.

Friends and sharing

If you invite a friend, the app uses iCloud sharing. The phone number or email address you enter is passed to Apple to deliver and verify the invitation.

Friends you connect with can see the upcoming visits you share with them: coordinates and dates, and the place name only if you allow it. Notes are never shared. You can turn off sharing per visit and per place. If you turn on Live Location, friends you select can see how far away you are; your exact position is not shown in the app.

To offer sharing back without a link, the app stores an invitation in the public CloudKit database. It contains your iCloud user identifier, your chosen display name and an encrypted link that only the invited friend can read. It is deleted once the invitation is accepted or withdrawn. You can remove friends at any time.

Exporting places

You can export selected places as a file and send it to others. The file contains names, addresses, coordinates, phone numbers, websites and tags. It never contains notes, ratings or visits. You decide whom you send it to.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Notifications

Notifications, for example about detected visits, potential meetups or nearby friends, are created on your device.

Your rights

Because your data is stored in your own iCloud, you can view, export and delete it at any time: in the app, under iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or by deleting the app. For questions or requests under the GDPR, contact us at jip@preuschl.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's handling of data changes. The current version is always available at this address.
